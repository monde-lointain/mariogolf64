1. **`lb` for val**: `s32 val = ((s8*)arg0)[0x2C]` (not `s8 val = *(s8*)`) — the s8

   default-char, no `-fsigned-char`.)
2. **single-exit v1 accumulator** (`goto end`) — cut the per-return `move`
