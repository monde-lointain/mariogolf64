## Root-caused pass (gcc-2.7.2 local-alloc.c)

REG_ALLOC_ORDER (numeric v0,v1,a0..). `(z/4)*8` and `x/4` are both single-ref

are coupled in a standalone expression -> unreachable by source form. Block3
avoids it naturally (x/4 is the gate value, created+emitted first, in v1).
